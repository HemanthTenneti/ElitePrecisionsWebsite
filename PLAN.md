# Elite Technologies site plan

## Source and scope

- Recreate the visible page structure, navigation, responsive layout and frontend interactions of `https://shaily.com/` with Next.js, Tailwind CSS and GSAP.
- Use the supplied Elite logo and `#12018A` as the primary brand color.
- Use placeholder visuals wherever the reference has an image or video.
- Use Elite's supplied company profile for company facts and contact information. Reference-site claims do not transfer to Elite unless supported by the profile.
- Frontend only: forms are usable UI with local confirmation; they do not submit data to a server.

## Pages

1. Shared navigation and footer; homepage.
2. Expertise overview; Healthcare; Consumer; Beauty and Personal Care; Lighting, Appliances and Automotive.
3. About; Vision, Mission & Values; Awards and Recognition; Capabilities; Quality & Compliance; Sustainability; Team.
4. Investors; Careers; Contact.
